This is an En Croissant classifier source milestone, adapter173 / live pipeline180. The separate Chess Mistake Trainer website, installed desktop and hosted phone app are not delivery targets of this verification. Existing owner data and unrelated checkout changes are preserved.

## A real trap created by closing escape squares

Public development puzzle Xg7Rd exposes a missing nomination rule. Bxg2 does not attack Na5 itself: Bc7 already attacks that knight. The move closes the formerly safe b7/c6 flights, while Nd2 controls the remaining escape. The all-defence proof must also answer counterattacks against those participating pieces. An unrelated loose piece cannot finance a trap claim.

Both-colour independent enumeration covers all 34 legal replies, including ...Bf3, ...Bh3 and ...Bb4. The local minimum is 320 centipawns, including the initial 100-point pawn capture and immediate liabilities. This is neither a free knight on top of that pawn nor a whole-position evaluation. Removing Nd2 permits ...Nc4 and must reject the trap. An already existing trap, a missing attacker, an unrelated queen liability and exhausted proof budgets remain negative controls.

The same 16 frozen contracts improve from 8 passing to 16. Colour reflection, input-length variants and constructed controls are not 16 independent public puzzles. Actual board evidence shows Bc7→Na5 and Bg2→b7/c6, never an invented Bg2→Na5 attack. See the trap-confinement receipts for independent proof, unchanged budgets and bounded nomination-cache checks.

## The preparing move takes priority over its later fork

Public development wN37d starts with Nb2. The knight attacks Ra4 and clears c4 for the rook's checking entry. A rook escape can be answered by Rc4+ and a fork, but the stronger ...Bd6 attacks Rc7 and requires the quiet repair Rc6. The new bounded proof closes every one of the 24 legal root replies, with all evasions of checking captures expanded before accepting a material-retention leaf.

The distinct look-alike Nb6 is rejected: ...Bd6 Rc6 Ra6 and a king evasion expose the later ...Rxb6 loss. Missing participants, pre-existing clear routes, clock claims, exhausted budgets and cache-mutation attempts are separate regression controls. No puzzle identity, engine score or source label is an admission condition.

The six paired public API contracts improve from 0 to 6 passing. The root-only, source-line and harder defensive-line variants in each colour are paired views of one public puzzle, not six independent examples. Primary is now Clearance Preparation with a conservative 180-centipawn local bound. The source line's later fork remains secondary at ply three, with its distinct local value, rather than becoming an invented first-move fork. Twenty-four additional source tests check the implementation contract and safeguards; baseline failures from a previously nonexistent helper are not counted as accuracy improvements.

## Later tactical evidence needs its own history

An independently constructed exact-FEN/history pair exposes a false later fork. Ne6+ at the root does not give the defender a repetition claim. Later Nc7+ does, so the displayed queen-winning fork and its subsequent payoff cannot be presented as forced consequences of that line. The clean history at the identical board and clocks retains them.

The new qualifier legally follows the supplied continuation, tracks actual claimants and checks local alternatives on their own reached board and move. It preserves independent root certificates, observations and drawing resources. A fresh scan after a player declines the claim can still show the now-available capture. An automatic termination cannot be revived by a later zeroing capture. Missing or mismatched history never manufactures a claim.

The fixed eight paired history/colour/prefix contracts improve from 6 passing to 8; four truncated variants already abstained. Independent Python-chess checks the history, each of five legal fork defences, the resulting queen capture and a safe alternate local move in both colours. This is one constructed mechanism, not eight independently sampled cases or a whole-position winning proof.

Reproduce with `python benchmarks/tactical-relevance/continuation-history-audit.py` and `node node_modules/vitest/vitest.mjs run --config benchmarks/tactical-relevance/continuation-history.vitest.ts`. To compare the frozen baseline set `CONTINUATION_HISTORY_REF=80155c616cf2dd0d45cb764c060d7e60b7df5ede`; optional `CONTINUATION_HISTORY_REPORT` must name a new output file. Source tests separately cover history binding, alternative routes, automatic termination and public classifier/live/review surfaces.

## Intermediate-capture research remains separate

The retained Ltbye audit closes bounded forward and reversed capture sequences, but does not relax the checking-only intermediate-capture admission rule. Bxe6 must be compared with immediate Qxc1, and the preceding rook loss plus later ...Qxa2 liability must not be hidden. Removing Rc8 is an opposite positive, not a valid negative: a bishop escape can leave time to retain the first captured bishop. An exhausted off-square-liability probe remains unknown. See the separate Ltbye review and receipts; this research is not a newly delivered intermezzo label.

The unchanged public cohort has 56 paired inputs from eight puzzles and six ordinary-game contexts. Only the two newly proved puzzle mechanisms change selected motif identities, values or priority in eight colour/input variants. The remaining 48 observations retain their selected motifs; this is not 48 certified correct negatives. No holdout or population-accuracy claim is made.

Final source verification passes 3,593 genuine regressions, with one separately counted known expected failure and 364 conditional skips. All 32 frozen primary-lesson outputs remain byte-identical. The exact staged core passes 772 checks with four skips independently of the pending checking-ray patch. These suites overlap and must not be added as independent positions. All 56 new source contracts, whole-project types, scoped lint and the frontend/review-worker build pass.

These finite legal/material certificates and regression contracts are not population accuracy estimates. History qualification remains path-specific outside mating proofs; it does not certify every branch of every material search as repetition-free. Unproved quiet preparations, intermediate captures, larger endgames and the known queen-ending promotion gap remain open. An unavailable tactical proof is not evidence that a position is positional.
