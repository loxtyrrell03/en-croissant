## Mechanisms and boundaries

- **KmpJY / QPNg8ZHh:** Qxg6+ has only Kf8. Nxg5 then creates Qf7#; every legal defence permits mate or a verified material concession. The child proof's 320 includes its initial capture; adding only the root pawn gives 420. Every outer reply needs its own connected child within one 8,192-operation budget. The original checker must be the child's mating partner. Captures, promotion debts, draw claims, terminal states and exhausted proofs cannot be ignored. The classifier says **Mating Attack Preparation**, keeping later mechanisms at their actual plies. This local proof alone cannot blame the preceding move: checking first may merely delay it.
- **Vk1Ud / cS8sT93s:** long castling checks with the relocated rook while the relocated king attacks Rb7. All seven legal replies lose that same rook to one of those two pieces. This is **Castling Double Attack**, not discovered check: the checking rook moved. Both standard and king-to-rook castling encodings must preserve the legal timeline and current-board arrows.
- **UohLn / Unhq2NQb:** Rxc8 uses Qf5 behind Pd7 to recapture after promotion. All four promotion roles, including off-square promotions, are checked separately. Promotion value is compensation, not an independent loose rook. The global promotion veto remains intact; only the connected, explicitly settled x-ray exchange gains the exception.
- **Retained checking forks:** both moves must independently prove the same original king and material targets, with exactly one checker: the landing forker. Different targets, discovered/double check and compound exchange proofs remain outside this comparison. Neutral context does not erase another independently proved cause. In the fixed Nd6 control, one line permits Kxd6 and the other does not; no equality or exact loss is inferred from differing lower bounds.

The independent pin/defensive audit is in `frontier-composition-adjudication.md`. ilrgH already has a truthful score-gated mating-threat observation; its supporting pin predates the move. Kn14A is a genuine defensive counterthreat whose full material proof remains unresolved. Neither empty output is silently relabelled positional, and neither source tag is treated as ground truth. The frontier's zugzwang and long queen-ending promotion gaps remain open.
