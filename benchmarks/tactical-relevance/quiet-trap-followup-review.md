# Queen confinement and promotion interference review

Two previously retained development cases contain concrete tactical mechanisms, but the evidence does not justify simply turning their source labels into root headlines. The queen-confinement case has a bounded material-positive answer to every legal defence. The promotion case has verified interference and a promotion double-check threat, while its complete root outcome remains unresolved. Neither result is a population accuracy measurement or a runtime change.

The original nominations and source-game links remain in `quiet-trap-adversarial-selection.json`. New hypotheses were fixed in `quiet-trap-followup-hypotheses.json` before these classifier/kernel queries. ## Queen confinement requires connected answers

For gMkbY, Black plays g6 from `r1bq3r/p4kp1/2p4p/2ppP1nQ/8/P1P2P2/1P4PP/RNB1R1K1 b - - 4 19`. It attacks Qh5. All 30 legal replies are enumerated, including e6+ and Qxg6+. Adapter175 emits no root motif. Its trap proof stops at Bxg5 with `Not a causal defender`; the separate single-root nominated-target query also remains unknown.

Bxg5 takes the knight that participates in the queen's confinement. The answer hxg5 recaptures that bishop and opens Rh8's file to the same queen. The bishop was not itself a defender of the queen, explaining why the existing defender-removal gate misses this continuation. In the displayed Bxg5 hxg5 Qxh8 Qxh8 branch, Black loses knight320 and rook500 and collects bishop330 and queen900, for a local net410. Those numbers are material units with a pawn worth100, not engine evaluations.

Qh4 requires a different connected answer: Nxf3+ both forks king and queen and uncovers Qd8's diagonal to h4. All four legal checking evasions permit a legal capture of Qh4. In particular, gxf3 removes the knight but leaves Qd8xh4. This is not a direct-capture-only trap.

Separate capped queries at a requested90 material threshold close all30 root defences in each colour, with a minimum returned bound100. Their total visit counts are20,670 and21,965; each individual defence has a16,384 cap. A separate400-threshold diagnostic closes29 of30 in each colour, but e6+ remains unknown without exhausting its cap. The weak bound may retain the sacrificed checking pawn without proving that the queen must fall. Consequently neither a universal410 gain nor inevitable queen capture is established. These are separate diagnostic budgets, not a claim that a complete runtime proof fits one existing budget. An initial shared16,384 probe exhausted before covering all defences.

The saved test replays every selected proof leaf, checks its material balance and verifies the complete next-defence set. The compact receipt retains root coverage, selected-leaf hashes, counts and examples; the deterministic test reconstructs the full local certificates. These certificates still use the existing kernel and are not independent whole-game proofs.

Two independent geometry controls constrain a future extension. Removing Rh8 permits Qh7 after g6 Bxg5 hxg5, with no immediate legal queen capturer. Adding a White pawn on f6 blocks Qd8's discovered diagonal: after g6 Qh4 Nxf3+ gxf3, no immediate legal capture of Qh4 remains. These controls refute those particular collections, not all possible tactics in the altered positions.

## The rook move interferes with a defender

For9YfbQ, Black plays Rd2 from `3r2k1/4bp2/p3p1pP/1bN1P1P1/1P3Q2/2P5/4pK2/2Rq3R b - - 5 35`. Pe2 blocks the rook's ray, so Rd2 is not check. There are39 legal White replies, including Qxf7+ and h7+.

The move has two exact effects. First, it blocks Qf4's defence of Rc1 along f4-e3-d2-c1. Immediate Qd1xc1 permits Qf4xc1; after Rd2 Kg3 Qxc1 that queen recapture is illegal, while Rh1xc1 remains legal. The actual reversed order therefore differs from a cooperative Rxc1 response. If Black tries Qxc1 Qxc1 Rd1, White has Rxd1 exd1=Q Qxd1, leaving Black down500 locally. The independent checker verifies the full legal sequence in both colours. Accepting the offered rook with Qxd2 instead allows Qxd2+, a local400 gain for Black.

Second, moving the rook behind Pe2 creates a promotion double check against Kf2. After28 of the39 actual replies, e1=Q is immediate checkmate. Ra1 is one explicit example. Promotion uncovers Rd2's attack while the new queen also checks. Before Rd2, immediate e1=Q+ permits Rh1xe1. Removing Pe2 is not a clean negative control: Rd2 then becomes a direct check, changing the mechanism.

The remaining11 replies are not proved losing by this audit. The full source line is still locally down300 for Black at its displayed endpoint; further collection must be established before calling it a material win. Root and full adapter175 classifications remain empty. Interference combined with a promotion threat is a supported causal description, but the strongest primary and complete root outcome remain unproved.

Ten fixed depth16 Stockfish queries provide corroboration only. They support Rd2 and expose the cooperative reversed-order mistake: the proper Qf4xc1 continuation favours White, unlike Rxc1. Adjacent-position scores differ materially at this finite search depth. No engine score is treated as an exact bound, a truth label or proof of uniqueness.

## Safe next proof boundaries

A trap extension could consider an actual counterattacker capturing a participating flight guard, then a recapture that opens a line to the same identified victim. It must charge the lost guard and all later liabilities, cover counterchecks and every reply, and preserve the common budget. A named trap must also distinguish collecting that victim from merely retaining a pawn sacrificed to buy an escape. Increasing a budget or accepting the displayed line alone would not resolve that semantic boundary.

For the rook case, an interference proof should bind the original legal Qf4-c1 guard, the actual blocker on d2 and the Qxc1 collection. A promotion proof must include its changed checking geometry and all eleven nonmating immediate defences, including checking moves and captures of the offered queen/rook. These are general proof requirements, not a recommendation to hard-code either position.
