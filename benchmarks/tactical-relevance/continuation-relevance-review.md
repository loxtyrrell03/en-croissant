# Continuation relevance and history-aware mating evidence

This milestone belongs to the **En Croissant fork**, adapter 172 / live pipeline 179. It does not change or deploy the separate Chess Mistake Trainer website. Source integration, generated-service checks and a frontend build are not installed desktop or phone verification.

## Preserve the win, correct the route and distance

An exact-FEN pair of legally constructed histories exposed a mate-distance error. Both positions were labelled mate in three. In one history, the proposed later Rc8+ permits a threefold-repetition claim; the root move itself does not. Independent enumeration refutes that three-move certificate, **not the move's winning result**.

The [independent history audit](history-aware-mate-adapter172.json) retains the all-reply checks, exact before/after API receipts and corrected adjudication. Across 16 paired history/colour/input-length variants, the distance contracts improve from 12 to 16 passing; this is not a 16-position independent accuracy sample.

A further all-defence search finds Qh6+ Kg8 Qxg6+ Kh8 Rc8+ Rf8 Rxf8#: the capture resets the reversible history and establishes a different mate in four. The classifier must retain this genuine win with its newly proved distance rather than erase all tactical evidence. A separate extra-rook control has a safe mate-in-three route through Rb8+ even though its supplied Rc8+ route repeats.

The new qualifier replays private, immutable history contexts and checks current/announced repetition claims at defensive nodes. It independently proves alternative routes, never treating a cooperative principal variation as sufficient proof. The existing seven-move / 65,536-node envelope remains bounded; successive distance attempts share the remaining allowance. Unsupported or exhausted proofs abstain, and do not turn into positional judgments. Missing or partial history is not a certificate that earlier repetitions did not occur.

Mating dependencies are explicit metadata, including value-less Mating Deflection. A replacement route proves a generic forcing mate, not the old route's named geometry, comparison or compound mechanism. Immediate completed mates, independently proved material tactics and drawing resources remain distinct. Conditional events are checked at their actual board and ply, with no automatic transfer to the root. Automatic seventy-five-move/fivefold termination cannot be revived by a subsequent resetting move.

## Connected mechanisms and irrelevant secondary attacks

The [mechanism review](connected-interference-discovery-review.md) and [paired receipt](connected-interference-discovery-receipt.json) distinguish two changes:

- On the reached GrHPv board, Qh8+ forces the king to block its queen's guard of h7. Qxh7+ then skewers that same queen against every reply. This independently proved interference takes priority over the smaller rook skewer; it does not certify the earlier quiet Ra1 preparation.
- Fresh development puzzle YvGsE retains mate-in-three and connected deflection, but loses an irrelevant later material-discovery badge. Removing both nonking joint-attack targets still permits the same forced mate. A continuation that actually captures the discovered queen retains its material explanation.

No global largest-value ranking rule, source-label shortcut or increased interference proof budget is used.

## Repeatable evidence, not a population accuracy claim

The [fresh cohort review](rare-causal-cohort-v2-review.md) records a frozen, output-blind selection across eight rare-motif strata and six fixed ordinary-game contexts. These yield paired input variants, not independent extra puzzles. Source tags are nominations, not truth labels; ordinary game positions are not automatically quiet negatives. One such context contains a genuine missed queen capture and is retained as a tactical-positive control.

The final [verification receipt](continuation-relevance-verification.json) records the complete source regression, frozen primary lessons, exact core snapshot excluding pre-existing checking-ray edits, compiled trainer export/reload tests, types, lint and build. Saved-card parsing preserves the new outcome dependency. The legacy cross-phase snapshot receives only a deterministic upgrade of its old mating metadata; its actual labels, values, explanations, board annotations and timelines remain independently compared.

Final verification: **3,537 genuine regression passes**, one separately counted known expected failure, 364 conditional skips and zero unexpected failures. All 32 frozen primary-lesson outputs are unchanged. The exact staged core passes 716 checks (four skips), the compiled trainer passes 77 (one optional engine check skipped), and the fresh cohort passes 14 narrow contracts. These selections overlap and must not be added together as independent examples. The 81 new source contracts include reflected/constructed controls, not 81 newly discovered puzzle defects. Whole-project types, 15-file lint and the 8,968-module frontend/review-worker build pass.

## Boundaries

The broader quiet-preparation, larger-ending and queen-ending promotion gaps remain. History-aware mate qualification is not history propagation through every material or endgame detector. Benchmark-only endgame composition is not enabled in the runtime. A rejected certificate never establishes that the whole move loses, and a passed regression never establishes general accuracy.

Owner games, practice progress, sessions, installed applications and hosted phone services are unchanged. Unrelated checkout work and the pending checking-ray patch are preserved. Build outputs are not committed.
