# Defensive counterthreats, outcome noise and saved-label provenance

Scope: En Croissant source, adapter 169 / live pipeline 176. No separate website migration, installed-app replacement, phone deployment or owner-data rescan is part of this milestone.

## Concrete changes

- **Kn14A / pxdMS4ar:** Nc3 legally interposes against the rook check and restores a mating threat. An explicit defensive proof covers all 27 replies and retains at least 180 cp of local material gain when mate is stopped. The knight costs 320 and the rook earns 500; the supposed ...Qxe4 compensation is independently refuted by Rxe8#. Checking continuations can require two king flights preserving the exact mating threat, followed by a safely captured checker. The existing 8,192-operation budget, promotion checks, draw boundaries and attacking-preparation gates remain intact. This is **Mating Attack**, not a forced-mate or whole-game-win claim. Thirty-eight regression controls include both colours, partial/full lines, missing partners, expensive interposers, losing flights, draw clocks and exhaustion. The independent certificate and selected-answer replay are retained separately.
- **Gtvlx / ugpVWplb:** Qxe3+ permits only fxe3 Bh4# or Qe2 Qxe2#. Mate in 2 remains primary. Its supporting **Mating Deflection** no longer receives a fabricated pawn value or says that mate is unproved. The classifier preserves a deliberately absent material value instead of filling it from the line's endpoint. Mixed mate/material deflections still report only their actual material lower bound.
- **sGGZN / I1scDAqN:** Rh8+ Kxh8 Qxh6+ gives a rook to collect the queen. The actual queen capture is **Deflection Payoff**, not an additional free queen. The mechanism and its payoff share the same guard-restoration counterfactual and all-reply proof. A newly viewed position obtains the same context only from complete, legal, bounded history reaching that exact FEN; invalid, incomplete, future or overlong history cannot invent compensation. No-history standalone captures retain their own local value.
- **Saved nature labels:** tactical/positional/unknown, confidence and reasons are read atomically only when both the nature algorithm and its motif-classifier dependency are current. A separate `natureMotifClassifierVersion` prevents a nature-only refresh from falsely stamping old motif arrays as current. Details, practice routing, counts and unrevealed views use the same rule. Large counts avoid synchronous proof work and show stale cards as unknown. Kn14A's new proof refreshes stale positional labels into tactical; unsupported cases remain unknown, while genuine quiet positional estimates remain available.
- **Draw-claim fallback:** the shared material-threat proof now checks current and announced fifty-move claims. This closes both a skewer and an accepted-deflection fallback on the same Rh8+ position. A later capturing reply cannot erase an earlier legal claim through a different quiet reply. Forced captures/pawn moves still reset the clock, and immediate checkmate keeps precedence.
