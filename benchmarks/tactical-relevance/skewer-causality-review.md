# Skewer relevance and defender removal validation

En Croissant adapter 175 and live pipeline 182 replace unsupported pawn-skewer labels with the actual defender-removal explanation in a reviewed public puzzle. They also preserve earlier exchange costs and prevent the later collection being counted as another gain. The shared mistake trainer passes saving, export and reload tests. This is a source milestone, not an installed desktop or phone update.

## Require the claimed skewer to earn material

A skewer with a non-king front piece now needs both the existing all-reply material certificate and a selected continuation in which that front piece moves and the attacking slider profitably captures the actual rear target. The latter gain includes recaptures and exposed friendly pieces; it cannot borrow credit from the initiating capture. The proof cache distinguishes the exact ray. King-front skewers and pins keep their existing rules.

In public development puzzle EpYOT, the former two skewer labels claimed captures that lose 800 or 230 material points on their own. They appeared profitable only after adding the queen already captured at the root. Controlled removal or relocation of the rear pawns preserves the real 320-point combination while keeping an unrelated rook capture blocked. Genuine skewers, including capture-initiated skewers, remain positive. A free queen capture is not rebranded as an incidental skewer. See the [independent causal audit](epyot-causal-review.md) for the legal replies, adverse controls and material limits.

## Preserve the actual cause and count its gain once

The correct lesson is to exchange the queen defending the knight before taking the knight. The old classifier already proved the material gain, but rejected defender removal as redundant with another attack. That supposedly independent attack itself relied on the same defender being removed.

The narrow correction requires the real defended target, an improved legal exchange, and a selected acceptance branch where the existing attacker collects that exact target. It reuses the already bounded, all-reply capture certificate; it does not expand the search limits. An equal or stronger, already-proved ordering lesson for the same target stays intact, including Qq0JW.

EpYOT now leads with Removing the Defender, valued at 320 from the root or 220 after including the preceding pawn loss. A pawn is worth 100 in these local material calculations; they are not engine evaluations or winning probabilities. The subsequent knight capture is a nonvalued payoff. Board arrows explain the old guard and actual collector, not the losing rear-pawn captures.

## Ordinary positions and verification

The [ordinary public-game audit](ordinary-precision-v1-review.md) retains 24 newly selected exact contexts from four reused public games and 94 played-line or quiet-alternative variants. It found no confirmed new false primary. Its two distinct positive root claims have independent legal support, and constructed opposite controls remain negative. Empty outputs are observations, not invented positional labels or evidence of complete recall. Adjacent positions and colour reflections are dependent cases, not independent games or a holdout.

- Broad regression: 3,687 genuine passes, one separately counted known expected promotion failure, 364 conditional skips and no unexpected failures across 248 selected files.
- All 32 frozen primary-lesson outputs are byte-identical to adapter 174. All 56 retained public-cohort outputs are also unchanged after excluding the version stamp.
- The exact staged core passes 901 tests with five skips across 42 files, excluding the preserved unrelated checking-ray patch.
- The rebuilt shared trainer passes 113 tests with one optional real-engine skip. Eight new EpYOT roundtrips cover short/full lines, both colours and prior-history presence.
- Whole-project types, scoped lint and frontend/shared-trainer builds pass. Existing build warnings remain. Generated artifacts are not committed.

The [verification receipt](skewer-causality-verification.json) records source hashes and evidence boundaries. Tests use tiny public or constructed fixtures and isolated temporary stores; no owner-store rescan, full-corpus copy, runtime restart, browser/native interaction or website change occurred.

## Remaining limits

These are finite local proofs and targeted development judgments, not a population accuracy estimate or a guarantee of correct labels in all chess positions. Missing or exhausted proof remains unknown. Longer quiet counterplay, broader recall and the known queen-ending promotion race remain open. The generic removal fallback still inherits the established guard and legal-exchange nomination; stricter direct-recapture adversarial coverage is a separate follow-up unless a concrete counterexample is established.
